WRI Urban Land Use dataset rasters are accessed via Google Earth Engine.

GEE asset: https://code.earthengine.google.com/?asset=projects/wri-datalab/urban_land_use/V1

Use the export script at:
3_comparitive_analysis/WRI/01_WRI_DataDownload.js

Place the exported per-country GeoTIFFs under data_external/wri_raw/{country}/*.tif

Reference: WRI/Guzder-Williams et al. (CEUS 2023, DOI: 10.1016/j.compenvurbsys.2022.101917)
