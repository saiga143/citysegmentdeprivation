Download the raw SSI source data from the SSI Zenodo record:
https://zenodo.org/records/14998570
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.14998570

Reference: Li, C., Yu, L., Ndugwa, R. et al. (Nature Cities 2025, DOI: 10.1038/s44284-025-00276-0)

After downloading, use the GEE export script at:
3_comparitive_analysis/SSI/01_SSI_DataRetrieval.js

Place the exported per-country rasters under data_external/ssi_raw/
(one {Country}_SSIpack100m.tif per country)

Note: The project Zenodo package (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20486977) contains derived
per-country clipped SSI rasters (data_external/zenodo/ssi_clipped/), not the
raw SSI source data.
